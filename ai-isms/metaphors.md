# Metaphors

Tip for writers and agents: do not strip out every metaphor. Flag the ones that could belong to almost anyone, anywhere, with no loss of meaning.

<details>
<summary><strong>AI-Assisted Writing and the Metaphor Problem</strong></summary>

AI writing tools are fluent, but fluency is not the same as originality or felt experience. One common tell is a reliance on safe, familiar metaphors. They sound literary enough to pass, but they often feel generic, emotionally thin, or detached from the character's actual world.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How to Spot a Weak Metaphor</strong></summary>

A metaphor is usually weak when it:

- Could appear in almost any genre or context
- Explains the feeling instead of evoking it
- Uses a familiar abstract image like weight, warmth, storm, darkness, or drowning
- Sounds poetic at first but leaves no clear image behind
- Pulls attention away from the scene instead of deepening it

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Common AI Metaphor Offenders</strong></summary>

**Light and Darkness**
- "A flicker of hope in the darkness"
- "Light pierced through the shadows of her doubt"
- "His eyes held a storm behind them"

**Weight and Burden**
- "The weight of the world on her shoulders"
- "Grief settled into her bones like lead"
- "He carried the burden of his past"

**Fire and Ice**
- "Her anger was a cold fire"
- "Ice ran through his veins"
- "A warmth bloomed in her chest"

**Water and Drowning**
- "She was drowning in her thoughts"
- "Emotions washed over him in waves"
- "He was barely keeping his head above water"

**Walls and Armor**
- "She had built walls around her heart"
- "His armor finally cracked"
- "She let her guard down for the first time"

**Threads and Weaving**
- "The threads of fate intertwined"
- "Their lives were woven together"

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How to contribute your own examples</strong></summary>

- Fork this repository.
- Open the file that contains the metaphor list.
- Add your example in the same format as the existing entries.
- If possible, include a short note explaining why the metaphor feels generic, overused, or distinctly AI-like.
- Save your changes with a clear commit message, such as `Add examples of common AI-writing metaphors`.
- Open a pull request to this repository.
- In your pull request, briefly describe what you added and why it belongs on the list.

**Contribution tips**

- Keep examples short.
- Add clearly cliched metaphors.
- Avoid duplicates unless your example shows a meaningful variation.

**Be respectful:** this list critiques patterns, not people.

</details>
