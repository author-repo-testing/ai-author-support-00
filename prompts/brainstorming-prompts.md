# Brainstorming Prompts

Tip: ask for a small set of options, add a constraint, and vary only one thing at a time.

1. Give me 5 possible story premises for this idea. Keep the genre fixed, but make the tone of each option distinct.

2. Brainstorm 5 complications for this character goal. Avoid stock twists, prophecy language, and generic "dark secret" reveals.

3. Generate 7 names for this setting. Include a one-line note on the mood each name suggests.

4. Give me 5 scene ideas that raise the stakes without adding violence or a new villain.

5. Brainstorm 5 ways to make this premise feel more specific to the protagonist's background, job, or worldview.

6. Give me 5 alternate directions for this scene. Keep the same core event, but vary pacing, tension, or emotional tone.
