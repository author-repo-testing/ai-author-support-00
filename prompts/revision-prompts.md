# Revision Prompts

Tip: make one kind of change at a time. Keep the author's intent fixed unless the prompt says otherwise.

1. Rewrite this paragraph to remove passive voice and strengthen the sensory detail without changing what happens.

2. Condense this exchange to no more than ten lines. Keep the tension, but let more of the meaning stay implied.

3. Revise this scene to sound more sarcastic and cynical without changing the actual events.

4. Highlight any clichéd metaphors in this passage and suggest three more specific alternatives for each one.

5. Recast this scene from the antagonist's first-person perspective while preserving the same plot beats.

6. Identify any AI-isms in this passage, such as overly formal phrasing, repeated sentence shapes, or generic emotional language. Suggest changes that sound more natural and specific.

7. Find weak, generic, or unnecessary metaphors in this passage. Remove them or rewrite them so they fit the character's perspective and setting.

8. Locate any triplets or list-of-three patterns in this passage. Suggest where to keep them, vary them, or cut them.

9. Analyze the sentence lengths in this passage. Rewrite a section to mix short, punchy sentences with longer, more flowing ones to create a better narrative rhythm and prevent a monotonous reading experience.
